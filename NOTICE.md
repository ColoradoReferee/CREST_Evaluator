The CMI (Critical Match Incident) and CRE (Clear Refereeing Error) classification framework follows the structure published by U.S. Soccer's professional refereeing evaluation methodology. Category names are used factually for interoperability with the official evaluation form; no verbatim handbook text is reproduced.
